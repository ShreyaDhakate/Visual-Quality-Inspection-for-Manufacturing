Workflow

Four stations, one pass down the line

Every part moves through the same sequence, in order — the way it would on a real conveyor.

01

Capture

A camera image of the part enters the pipeline, uploaded or pulled from the line.

02

Preprocess

The frame is normalized and cropped so the model sees the part consistently.

03

Detect

The vision model scans for cracks, scratches, missing parts, and misalignment.

04

Report

A pass/fail result is stamped with defect type, location, and confidence.

Detection runs on-device · results are session-local
